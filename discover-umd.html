Why I Chose UMD

Location

Growing up, I visited Duluth several times, especially during the winter to experience Bentleyville and all the lights. As I got older, my dad and I would take trips to Duluth to walk around Canal Park and enjoy the beautiful weather.

Academic Programs

I was drawn to UMD's strong academic programs, especially in the Communications program. The opportunity to study in a smaller, more intimate setting while still having access to top-tier resources and faculty made UMD an ideal fit for me.

The price

I was drawn to UMD's affordable tuition and financial aid opportunities. A s a student, I value the accessibility of higher education and UMD's commitment to making college affordable for students like me.

What I Love About Duluth

I was drawn to Duluth because of its unique blend of natural beauty and vibrant culture. Living just blocks from Lake Superior means I can enjoy stunning waterfront views and outdoor activities year-round. The city's iconic Aerial Lift Bridge and bustling Canal Park area offer a lively atmosphere with great restaurants, shops, and entertainment. Plus, the abundance of hiking trails, ski slopes, and parks makes it easy to stay active and connected to nature while still being part of a close-knit community.

Campus & City Highlights

Enger Tower

Enger Tower is one of my favorite spots in the city. It offers panoramic views of Duluth and Lake Superior, making it a perfect place to relax and take in the scenery. I love going there with friends to watch the sunset or just enjoy the fresh air between classes. It also helps to get a good workout in.

Library

The library is a quiet and inspiring place to study and research. It's equipped with modern facilities and has a great collection of books and resources. My freshman year I spent a lot of time studying there, and I appreciated how it provided a focused environment while still being a welcoming space to collaborate with classmates or meet up with friends for a study break.

Canal Park

Canal Park is a beautiful and popular spot in Duluth. It features walking trails, a lake, and plenty of green space for relaxation. I love spending time there during the warmer months, especially walking on the boardwalk or just enjoying the peaceful surroundings.

Vitta Pizza

Vitta Pizza is one of my favorite local restaurants in Duluth. They have delicious pizza and a cozy atmosphere that's perfect for hanging out with friends after class or on the weekends. I love trying their different pizza flavors and enjoying the friendly service.

My UMD Experience

Living in Duluth has been an incredible experience. The combination of a supportive campus community and the stunning natural surroundings has made my time at UMD truly special. I've had the opportunity to explore the city, make lifelong friends, and grow both academically and personally. Whether it's studying by the lake, attending campus events, or just enjoying the beauty of Duluth, I feel grateful to call this place home during my college years.
