My Passions & Interests

Formula 1

In 2020, I discovered Formula 1 before the pandemic shut everything down. As time went on, I started watching more races and following each team and driver closely. One day durning lock down, I found drive to Survive on Netflix and binged the five season series in about two weeks. The show and the social media attention around F1 made me fall in love with the sport.

Towards the end of high school, I realized I needed to figure out what I wanted to do with my life. I had always been interested in Photography but I knew I would not need to attend college to continue my passion for the art. After doing a lot of research, I decided that I wanted to pursue a career in F1. I would be able to grow my love for the sport and be able to use my creativity and people skills to work in the industry.

On the day of my high school graduation, my parents surpised me with tickets to the F1 race in Austin, Texas. I had been dreaming of attending an F1 race since I first discovered the sport, and being able to attend the race with my parents only made my love for the sport grow even more.

Listening to music

From blasting Queen in the backseat of my step dad's truck to screaming out lyrics at my favorite concert venue. I have always been passionate about music. I have been lucky enough to attend more that 15 concerts and I am planning many more. I currently have three concerts planned for this year being, but if my favorite artists announce a tour, I will do whatever I can to get tickets. Ticketmaster fears me.

Once I had my own spending money, I started collecting vinyl records. My most prized posession is my original print of Fleetwood Mac's Rumors album. I have been able to get some great records at my local record shop and some that have been passed down or gifted to me by my family.

I have always had a passion for music. I participated in choir all through middle school and high school. My freshmen year of high school I participated in show choir and continued to do so until I graduated. In choir I competed at solo and ensemble competitions. I had great opportunities to perform and grow my love for music.
